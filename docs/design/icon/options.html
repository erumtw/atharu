Atharu icon options

Nine Liquid Glass directions for the Mac app icon. A–C are the first round; D–F lean further into Japanese craft; G–I are sumi ink on washi, following D but with no red seal. Each card shows the icon large, at Dock-to-favicon sizes on light and dark wallpaper, and as the one-colour menu-bar icon.

Chosen · Option I, Dry brush

Four looks you can switch between in the app

Sumi is plain washi. Sora and Wakaba dye the paper in your mint, sky and teal reference tone and keep the ink deep, so the brush still reads at 16 px. Glass turns the brush itself into Apple-style Liquid Glass. The menu-bar icon stays one colour, because macOS tints it.

All options

Decision

Option I, Dry brush, in four switchable looks: Sumi (default), Sora (blue), Wakaba (green) and Glass. The app gets an icon picker, and the menu-bar mark and favicon follow the same shape.
